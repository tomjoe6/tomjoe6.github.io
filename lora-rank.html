LoRA 的 r 怎么定

结论是没有准确公式🉑算，只能大致扫/估计

秩与奇异值谱

一个 \(d\times k\) 的矩阵有 \(\min(d,k)\) 个奇异值。秩等于非零奇异值的个数，回答有几个非零方向。

以 \(d=k=5120\) 的层为例，\(\Delta W\) 有 5120 个奇异值。全参微调之后通常是满秩的：5120 个奇异值全部非零，只是大多数极小。Biderman et al. 2024 在代码、数学任务上测到的 \(\Delta W\) 谱就是这种样子，衰减很慢。所以在这个尺度上问秩是多少得不到任何信息，有信息的是衰减速度。

式 1\[\begin{aligned}&\Delta W=W_{\mathrm{ft}}-W_0=U\Sigma V^{\top},\\ &\Sigma=\operatorname{diag}(\sigma_1,\dots,\sigma_{\min(d,k)}),\\ &\sigma_1\ge\sigma_2\ge\cdots\ge\sigma_{\min(d,k)}\ge 0,\\ &\operatorname{rank}(\Delta W)=\bigl|\{\,i:\sigma_i>0\,\}\bigr|.\end{aligned}\]

这里 \(U\) 和 \(V\) 的列分别是左右奇异向量，\(\sigma_i\) 是奇异值。秩只看 \(\sigma_i\) 是否为零，这正是它在微调场景里失效的原因。

LoRA

LoRA 把这层改动限制在低秩乘积上，见式 2。

式 2\[W=W_0+\frac{\alpha}{r}BA,\qquad B\in\mathbb{R}^{d\times r},\quad A\in\mathbb{R}^{r\times k},\quad r\ll\min(d,k).\]

初始化通常取 \(A\sim\mathcal{N}(0,\sigma^2)\)、\(B=0\)，于是 \(\Delta W=0\)，训练从原模型出发，起点没有扰动。约束来自一行代数，见式 3。

式 3\[\operatorname{rank}(\Delta W)=\operatorname{rank}(BA)\le\min(\operatorname{rank}A,\operatorname{rank}B)\le r.\]

r 设小了，最优解落在可达集合之外，怎么训都训不到。但训不到并不等于差，只要被丢掉的那些方向对 loss 贡献极小就无所谓。

截断 SVD 与能量占比

由 Eckart–Young 定理，截断 SVD 是最优的秩不超过 r 的近似，无论用 Frobenius 范数还是谱范数衡量，见式 4。

式 4\[\begin{aligned}\|\Delta W-\Delta W_r\|_F&=\min_{\operatorname{rank}(M)\le r}\|\Delta W-M\|_F=\Bigl(\sum_{i>r}\sigma_i^2\Bigr)^{1/2},\\ \|\Delta W-\Delta W_r\|_2&=\sigma_{r+1}.\end{aligned}\]

于是前 r 项的能量占比，见式 5。

式 5\[E(r)=\frac{\sum_{i\le r}\sigma_i^2}{\sum_{i=1}^{\min(d,k)}\sigma_i^2}=1-\frac{\|\Delta W-\Delta W_r\|_F^2}{\|\Delta W\|_F^2}\]

它衡量秩 r 能保留多少改动。

有全参 ckpt 时的直接测量

逐层算 \(\Delta W=W_{\mathrm{ft}}-W_0\)，做 SVD，得到奇异值谱 \(\sigma_1\ge\dots\ge\sigma_{\min(d,k)}\ge 0\)，画 \(E(r)\) 整条曲线，不要只看一个 r 点。

这个谱用于判断要不要 LoRA。根据 Biderman et al. 2024 在代码、数学任务上的测量，4096×4096 的 \(W_q\) 要 1500 以上的秩才解释 \(\Delta W\) 90% 的方差，比常用 LoRA 秩大一两个数量级；同一批实验里，他们给出的实用建议是按显存约束挑 r，16 配全部模块是个好起点。Shuttleworth et al. 2024 测到的全参更新有效秩也在 400 以上。所以谱衰减慢不是「r 该取大」的信号，而是这个任务上 LoRA 追不上全参。

\(E(r)\) 数的是能量，谱上大的方向对任务可以并不重要，真实的 loss 增量由曲率决定，二阶近似下是 \(\tfrac12\sum_{i>r}h_i\sigma_i^2\)，\(h_i\) 是 loss 在方向 i 上的曲率。所以拿 \(E(r)\) 反推 r 得不到🉑用的数。

\(E(r)\) 高只说明全参解可以被低秩逼近：根据 Shuttleworth et al. 2024，低秩下 LoRA 学出的更新与全参谱不同，会出现与预训练谱近似正交的新方向，有效秩不到全参的一半。所以最后仍要在 held-out 上确认。

甜点在哪

r 往上加表现并非一直变好。欠秩时丢掉被截掉的那些方向，截断误差降不下来；过了某个点，它已经接近零，而每多一维秩要多付一份方差，量级是 \(r\cdot d/n\)。

Arunan 2026 把这个写成了界：目标更新有一个固有的秩，\(r\) 小于它时误差卡在截断项上，\(r\) 大于它时按 \(r\cdot d/n\) 线性涨——曲线是 U 形，过了甜点不只是不涨，是会掉。他们跑了 168 组 DistilBERT、RoBERTa 的微调，验证 loss 都是这个形状，其中两个 SST-2 配置在大秩上掉得统计显著。这条只对固定秩的经验风险最小化解成立；换成核范数一类会自动收敛到那个秩的估计器，过秩就无害了。所以实测看到的平台，有一部分是训练过程在替你兜底。

准确位置算不出来。U 形的底很大程度由任务的谱定，不由模型结构定。实测也是这么回事：GPT-3 175B 上 WikiSQL 的准确率从 r=1 到 r=64 基本不动（Hu et al. 2021）；同一个 Llama-2-7B，math 上指令微调到 r=256 才追平全参，持续预训练连 r=256 都追不上（Biderman et al. 2024）。结构一样时，任务一换，甜点差两个数量级。

内在维度与早期谱代理

内在维度法。把更新限制在一个随机 d 维子空间里训至收敛，画 loss–d 曲线找拐点，见式 6。

式 6\[\theta=\theta_0+P\theta',\qquad P\in\mathbb{R}^{D\times d},\quad d\ll D,\qquad d^{*}=\min\bigl\{\,d:\ R(d)\ge 0.9\,R_{\mathrm{full}}\,\bigr\},\]

其中 \(R\) 是验证集指标，\(R_{\mathrm{full}}\) 是全参微调的结果。对应到 LoRA，有一个简单得多的做法：固定随机初始化的 \(A\)、只训 \(B\)，取最小的 r 使 held-out 达到 0.9 \(R_{\mathrm{full}}\)（判据同式 6）。\(\Delta W=BA\) 的行空间被限制在 \(A\) 的 r 维行空间内，可训练参数量 \(d\cdot r\)。根据 Zhu et al. 2024，只训 B 比只训 A 有效，随机不训练的 A 和训过的 A 差不多；FLoRA（Hao et al. 2024）把这个更新近似成对梯度做一次随机投影。成本中等。

早期谱代理。全参先跑 200 到 500 步，对累计更新做 SVD，见式 7。

式 7\[S_T=\sum_{t=1}^{T}\Delta W_t,\qquad \Delta W_t=W_t-W_{t-1},\]

再对 \(S_T\) 套用式 5 计算 \(E(r)\)。以 SGD 为例 \(\Delta W_t=-\eta_t g_t\)，用 Adam 一类优化器时换成对应的更新量即可。这条路的先例都是拿谱做初始化，不是拿它定 r——SLoRA（Babakniya et al. 2023）用一段全参后的 \(\Delta W\) 初始化 LoRA；EVA（Paischer et al. 2024）用激活的增量 SVD 初始化 A，并按解释方差把给定的秩预算分到各层（它的谱来自初始化时的激活，不是训了几百步的 \(\Delta W\)）。

跑这几百步比起跑完，代价🉑以忽略；但拿到的谱只能当粗估。

模块选择、α 缩放与逐层 r

模块选择。QLoRA（Dettmers et al. 2023）消融出来的结论是把 adapter 加到全部线性层（含 FFN），只配 q、v 会明显掉质量——覆盖面比秩更关键。所以同等参数量下，“全部线性层配小 r”优于“只用 q、v 配大 r”：参数量相同不代表表达能力相同，前者可选的更新方向更多，只是每个方向更窄。

α 的缩放。真正生效的是 \(\alpha/r\)，见式 8。

式 8\[s=\frac{\alpha}{r},\qquad \Delta W=s\,BA.\]

Hu et al. 2021 用的是 \(\alpha/r\) 这个缩放，常取 \(\alpha=2r\)，使有效尺度 \(s\) 恒为 2。改 r 必须同步改 α，否则等于偷偷改了学习率——把 r 从 \(r_1\) 换到 \(r_2\) 而 α 不变，更新整体乘上 \(r_1/r_2\)。r 很大时 \(\alpha/r\) 会偏小：Kalajdzievski 2023 把缩放换成 \(\alpha/\sqrt{r}\)；Shuttleworth et al. 2024 测到固定 α 配高秩会引入更多与预训练谱正交的新方向，换成 \(\alpha/\sqrt{r}\) 后高秩 LoRA 才更像全参。

逐层 r。\(E(r)\) 要按层分别算。根据 Biderman et al. 2024，首尾层的秩偏低、中间层偏高，MLP 比 attention 高；不过手调的逐层配置收益往往不抵复杂度——按解释方差在预算内自动分配（EVA）是同预算下权衡更好的一条路。

训后参与比校验

对训好的 \(BA\) 做 SVD，算参与比，见式 9。

式 9\[\mathrm{PR}=\frac{\bigl(\sum_{i=1}^{r}\sigma_i\bigr)^2}{\sum_{i=1}^{r}\sigma_i^2}\in[1,r].\]

奇异值分布均匀时它约等于 r，集中在单一方向时趋近 1。训完还能砍秩这件事有先例：AdaLoRA（Zhang et al. 2023）在训练中按重要度给各层分配并裁剪秩，低预算下优于固定秩。所以参与比远小于 r 就可以缩，奇异值尾部仍然很大就该加。这一步能把冗余直接换成推理上的节省：权重合并进 \(W\) 后推理没有额外成本，但在多套 adapter 并存、不合并权重时，开销与 r 成正比。

结论

到头来很大程度还是看硬件资源

先算 r 的成本上界。适配器的参数量是 r·Σ_l(d_l + k_l)，反过来就是 r ≤ 预算 / Σ_l(d_l + k_l)。Llama-3-8B 的规模（32 层、隐藏 4096、FFN 14336、7 个线性层）Σ(d+k) 约 2.8M：预算给到全参的 0.5%（约 45M 参数），除下来 r ≈ 16。Biderman et al. 2024 说的“按显存约束挑 r”就是这个意思——在这个范围里，r 是成本调节，16 配全部模块就是他们试过的配置里给的推荐。

r 的下界没有式子，只能按任务给量级：数据越多、离预训练分布越远，需要的秩越高。所以可以默认全模块 r = 16，α 同步取 2r。要更准，就在这个范围里把 r 定在 held-out 达到全参九成的那一点（式 6）：有全参 ckpt 就冻结随机 A、只训 B；没有就把更新限制在随机 d 维子空间里扫。

训完用参与比收尾，远小于 r 就减，奇异值尾部还大就加。手调的逐层配置通常不抵复杂度。所有数字最后都要在 held-out 上过一遍。

How to Choose LoRA's Rank r

The conclusion: there is no exact formula, only a rough scan or estimate.

Rank and the singular-value spectrum

A \(d\times k\) matrix has \(\min(d,k)\) singular values. The rank equals the number of nonzero singular values, and it answers how many nonzero directions there are.

Take a layer with \(d=k=5120\). Then \(\Delta W\) has 5120 singular values. After full fine-tuning it is usually full rank: all 5120 are nonzero, but most of them are tiny. Biderman et al. 2024 measure exactly this slow decay in \(\Delta W\) spectra on code and math tasks. Asking "what is the rank" at this scale carries no information. The decay rate does.

Equation 1\[\begin{aligned}&\Delta W=W_{\mathrm{ft}}-W_0=U\Sigma V^{\top},\\ &\Sigma=\operatorname{diag}(\sigma_1,\dots,\sigma_{\min(d,k)}),\\ &\sigma_1\ge\sigma_2\ge\cdots\ge\sigma_{\min(d,k)}\ge 0,\\ &\operatorname{rank}(\Delta W)=\bigl|\{\,i:\sigma_i>0\,\}\bigr|.\end{aligned}\]

Here the columns of \(U\) and \(V\) are the left and right singular vectors, and \(\sigma_i\) are the singular values. Rank only looks at whether each \(\sigma_i\) is zero. That is precisely why it fails as a tool in this setting.

LoRA

LoRA restricts the update to a low-rank product:

Equation 2\[W=W_0+\frac{\alpha}{r}BA,\qquad B\in\mathbb{R}^{d\times r},\quad A\in\mathbb{R}^{r\times k},\quad r\ll\min(d,k).\]

Initialization usually takes \(A\sim\mathcal{N}(0,\sigma^2)\) and \(B=0\), so \(\Delta W=0\) and training starts from the pretrained function with no perturbation. The constraint then follows from one line of algebra:

Equation 3\[\operatorname{rank}(\Delta W)=\operatorname{rank}(BA)\le\min(\operatorname{rank}A,\operatorname{rank}B)\le r.\]

If r is too small, the optimum lies outside the reachable set, and no amount of training gets there. But not getting there is not the same as being worse: as long as the discarded directions contribute very little to the loss, it does not matter.

Truncated SVD and the energy ratio

By the Eckart–Young theorem, the truncated SVD is the optimal approximation of rank at most r in both the Frobenius and the spectral norm:

Equation 4\[\begin{aligned}\|\Delta W-\Delta W_r\|_F&=\min_{\operatorname{rank}(M)\le r}\|\Delta W-M\|_F=\Bigl(\sum_{i>r}\sigma_i^2\Bigr)^{1/2},\\ \|\Delta W-\Delta W_r\|_2&=\sigma_{r+1}.\end{aligned}\]

The retained energy fraction is therefore

Equation 5\[E(r)=\frac{\sum_{i\le r}\sigma_i^2}{\sum_{i=1}^{\min(d,k)}\sigma_i^2}=1-\frac{\|\Delta W-\Delta W_r\|_F^2}{\|\Delta W\|_F^2}\]

which measures how much of the change rank r preserves.

Measuring directly with a full checkpoint

Compute \(\Delta W=W_{\mathrm{ft}}-W_0\) layer by layer, take the SVD to get \(\sigma_1\ge\dots\ge\sigma_{\min(d,k)}\ge 0\), and plot the whole \(E(r)\) curve rather than a single rank.

This spectrum is for deciding whether to use LoRA. Per Biderman et al. 2024, measured on code and math tasks, a \(4096\times4096\) \(W_q\) needs a rank above 1500 to explain 90% of the variance in \(\Delta W\), one to two orders of magnitude above typical LoRA ranks; on the same setup their practical recommendation is to size r against memory, with 16 across all modules a good starting point. Shuttleworth et al. 2024 measure effective ranks above 400 for full fine-tuning updates. So a slowly decaying spectrum is not a signal to raise r — it means LoRA trails full fine-tuning on this task.

\(E(r)\) counts energy, a direction that is large in the spectrum can matter little for the task, and the real change in loss is set by curvature, \(\tfrac12\sum_{i>r}h_i\sigma_i^2\) to second order, where \(h_i\) is the curvature of the loss along direction i. So inverting \(E(r)\) does not give a usable rank.

A high \(E(r)\) only says the full-fine-tuning solution can be approximated at low rank: per Shuttleworth et al. 2024, at low rank LoRA learns an update whose spectrum differs from full fine-tuning, with new directions nearly orthogonal to the pretrained spectrum and an effective rank under half of full fine-tuning's. So still confirm on held-out data.

Where the sweet spot is

Adding rank does not always improve things. Under-ranking leaves the truncated directions on the table and that truncation error will not come down; past some point it is already near zero, while every extra unit of rank costs another unit of variance, on the order of \(r\cdot d/n\).

Arunan 2026 turns this into bounds: the target update has an intrinsic rank, and excess error sits on the truncation term below it and grows linearly as \(r\cdot d/n\) above it — the curve is U-shaped, so past the sweet spot it does not merely stop improving, it gets worse. Across 168 DistilBERT and RoBERTa fine-tuning runs the validation loss traced that U, with two SST-2 configurations degrading significantly at large rank. This holds for a fixed-rank empirical risk minimizer; use an estimator that converges on that rank by itself, such as a nuclear-norm penalty, and over-ranking is harmless. So part of the plateau people measure is the training procedure covering for them.

The exact location is still not computable. The bottom of the U is set largely by the task's spectrum, not by the model's structure. Measurements agree: on GPT-3 175B, WikiSQL accuracy barely moves from r=1 to r=64 (Hu et al. 2021); on one Llama-2-7B, math instruction tuning needs r=256 to match full fine-tuning while continued pretraining does not catch up even there (Biderman et al. 2024). Same structure, different task, sweet spots two orders of magnitude apart.

Intrinsic dimension and the early spectral proxy

The intrinsic-dimension method. Restrict the update to a random d-dimensional subspace, train to convergence, and look for the knee of the loss–d curve:

Equation 6\[\theta=\theta_0+P\theta',\qquad P\in\mathbb{R}^{D\times d},\quad d\ll D,\qquad d^{*}=\min\bigl\{\,d:\ R(d)\ge 0.9\,R_{\mathrm{full}}\,\bigr\},\]

where \(R\) is a validation metric and \(R_{\mathrm{full}}\) is the full fine-tuning result. Translated back to LoRA, it reduces to something much cheaper: freeze a randomly initialized \(A\), train only \(B\), and pick the smallest r whose held-out score reaches 0.9 \(R_{\mathrm{full}}\) (same criterion as Equation 6). The row space of \(\Delta W=BA\) is confined to the \(r\)-dimensional row space of \(A\), with \(d\cdot r\) trainable parameters. Per Zhu et al. 2024, training B alone is more effective than training A alone, and a random untrained A performs nearly as well as a fine-tuned one; FLoRA (Hao et al. 2024) approximates this update as a random projection of the gradient. Moderate cost.

The early spectral proxy. Run full fine-tuning for 200–500 steps and take the SVD of the accumulated update:

Equation 7\[S_T=\sum_{t=1}^{T}\Delta W_t,\qquad \Delta W_t=W_t-W_{t-1},\]

then apply Equation 5 to \(S_T\) to get \(E(r)\). Under SGD, \(\Delta W_t=-\eta_t g_t\); with Adam-style optimizers, substitute the corresponding update. The precedents for this route all use a spectrum for initialization rather than to set r — SLoRA (Babakniya et al. 2023) initializes LoRA from the SVD of a short full fine-tuning run; EVA (Paischer et al. 2024) initializes A from an incremental SVD of activations and uses the explained variance to split a given rank budget across layers (its spectrum comes from activations at initialization, not from a \(\Delta W\) measured a few hundred steps in).

A few hundred steps are negligible next to a full run, but the spectrum they give you is only a rough estimate.

Target modules, α scaling, and per-layer rank

Target modules. The ablation result from QLoRA (Dettmers et al. 2023) is to put adapters on all linear layers including the FFN — q and v alone lose noticeable quality. Coverage matters more than rank. So at equal parameter budget, "all linear layers with a small r" beats "q and v only with a large r": equal parameter count is not equal expressive capacity, the broader configuration has more directions available, each a narrower one.

The α scaling. What actually takes effect is \(\alpha/r\):

Equation 8\[s=\frac{\alpha}{r},\qquad \Delta W=s\,BA.\]

Hu et al. 2021 use the \(\alpha/r\) scaling; the common choice \(\alpha=2r\) holds the effective scale \(s\) at 2. If you change r you must change α with it, or you have quietly changed the learning rate: moving from \(r_1\) to \(r_2\) with α fixed multiplies the whole update by \(r_1/r_2\). For very large r, \(\alpha/r\) becomes small: Kalajdzievski 2023 replaces the scaling with \(\alpha/\sqrt{r}\), and Shuttleworth et al. 2024 measure that a fixed α at high rank introduces more directions orthogonal to the pretrained spectrum — with \(\alpha/\sqrt{r}\), high-rank LoRA resembles full fine-tuning much more closely.

Per-layer rank. Read \(E(r)\) for each layer separately. Per Biderman et al. 2024, first and last layers rank lower and middle layers higher, and MLP modules rank above attention; but hand-tuned per-layer configuration rarely repays its complexity — allocating the budget automatically by explained variance (EVA) is a better trade-off at the same budget.

Checking the trained adapter

Take the SVD of the trained \(BA\) and compute the participation ratio:

Equation 9\[\mathrm{PR}=\frac{\bigl(\sum_{i=1}^{r}\sigma_i\bigr)^2}{\sum_{i=1}^{r}\sigma_i^2}\in[1,r].\]

It is approximately r when the singular values are spread evenly, and approaches 1 when they concentrate in a single direction. There is precedent for cutting rank after training: AdaLoRA (Zhang et al. 2023) allocates and prunes rank across layers by importance during training, and beats a fixed rank at low budget. So a participation ratio well below r means r can be reduced, and a heavy tail means r is binding and should be raised. This step turns redundancy directly into inference savings: merging the adapter into \(W\) removes inference cost entirely, but when several adapters must be served unmerged, cost scales with r.

Conclusion

In the end, it largely comes down to hardware.

Start with the cost upper bound on r. The adapter holds r·Σl(dl+kl) parameters, so r ≤ budget / Σl(dl+kl). At Llama-3-8B scale (32 layers, hidden 4096, FFN 14336, seven linear layers) Σ(d+k) is about 2.8M: give the adapter 0.5% of the full model (about 45M parameters) and r comes out at 16. That is what Biderman et al. 2024 mean by sizing r against memory — within this range r is a cost parameter, and 16 across all modules is the recommendation from the configurations they tested.

The lower bound on r has no formula; only the task gives an order of magnitude — more data and a task further from pretraining both push the needed rank up. So a reasonable default is r = 16 across all modules, with α = 2r. For more precision, set r inside that range at the point where held-out reaches 90% of full fine-tuning (Equation 6): with a full checkpoint, freeze a random A and train only B; without one, train inside a random d-dimensional subspace instead.

After training, finish with the participation ratio: well below r, shrink; tail still large, grow. Hand-tuned per-layer configuration rarely repays its complexity. And run every number past held-out in the end.
