电容阵列 SAR ADC 的简单原理

电容阵列 SAR ADC 具有高精度、小面积的优点。它的基本思路是二分法逐次逼近，而电路实现的关键在于电容两端电荷守恒和下极板开关切换。

基本结构

在一种常见结构中，电容阵列的上极板连接输入端 Vin 和比较器一端，下极板通过开关在 GND 与 VDD 之间切换。SAR 逻辑根据比较器输出逐位决定各电容下极板的状态，最终得到数字输出。

电荷守恒与逐次逼近

电路实现中，电容两端电荷量 Q = CU 守恒，因此可以通过改变下极板电压来调节上极板电压，并将其送到比较器与 0.5VDD 比较。

假设电容阵列为 4C, 2C, C, C，VDD = 1.8V，Vin = 0.6V。先让所有电容下极板接地、上极板接入 Vin，此时电容两端电压锁定在 0.6V。

断开 Vin 后，将 4C 的下端拨至 VDD。根据所有电容的电荷守恒，有：

Q = 8C · Vin = 4C · (Vup - VDD) + 4C · Vup

因此可以得到：

Vup = Vin + (4 / 8) · VDD

这是第一次二分。此时 V- = 1.5V，大于 0.5VDD = 0.9V，因此 4C 下端会回到 0。继续逐位尝试后，当 Vup = Vin + (1 / 8) · VDD 时可以取得小于 0.5VDD 的最大值。结合电容开关状态和数学变换，就能得到 Vin 的二进制序列表示。

不同结构的共同点

也有其他类型的 SAR ADC 中，比较器正负端并不是简单的 0.5VDD 与 Vin + 电容阵列。例如比较器两端可以分别接 Vin 与电容阵列。虽然连接方式不同，但底层原理仍然一致：通过电荷守恒、电容阵列 DAC 和比较器反馈实现逐次逼近。

A Simple Introduction to Capacitor-Array SAR ADCs

Capacitor-array SAR ADCs are attractive because they can provide high precision with small area. The key idea is binary-search-like successive approximation, implemented through charge conservation and bottom-plate switching.

Basic Structure

In a common structure, the top plates of the capacitor array connect to Vin and one input of the comparator, while the bottom plates are switched between GND and VDD. SAR logic uses comparator feedback to decide each capacitor switch state and finally produces a digital output.

Charge Conservation and Successive Approximation

In circuit implementation, capacitor charge follows Q = CU. By changing the bottom-plate voltage, the top-plate voltage can be adjusted and compared against 0.5VDD.

Assume a capacitor array of 4C, 2C, C, C, with VDD = 1.8V and Vin = 0.6V. First, all bottom plates are connected to ground and the top plate samples Vin, so the capacitor voltage is locked at 0.6V.

After disconnecting Vin, switch the bottom plate of 4C to VDD. By charge conservation:

Q = 8C · Vin = 4C · (Vup - VDD) + 4C · Vup

Therefore:

Vup = Vin + (4 / 8) · VDD

This is the first binary-search step. Now V- = 1.5V, which is larger than 0.5VDD = 0.9V, so the bottom plate of 4C returns to 0. After continuing bit by bit, Vup = Vin + (1 / 8) · VDD gives the largest value below 0.5VDD. By analyzing capacitor switching and the mathematical transformation, the binary representation of Vin can be obtained.

Common Principle Across Variants

In some SAR ADC structures, the comparator inputs are not simply 0.5VDD and Vin + capacitor array. For example, the positive and negative inputs may connect to Vin and the capacitor array separately. Even with different connections, the underlying principle remains the same: successive approximation through charge conservation, a capacitor-array DAC, and comparator feedback.
